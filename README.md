# Datheon – Task 3: Customer Cohort & Churn Analytics

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer retention and churn behavior using the IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset. The objective is to identify customer segments with high churn rates, understand retention patterns, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer loyalty.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall customer churn and retention rates.
- Segment customers based on tenure cohorts.
- Evaluate churn patterns across contract types and payment methods.
- Analyze the relationship between monthly charges and customer churn.
- Identify high-risk customer segments.
- Develop actionable customer retention strategies.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Plotly
- Jupyter Notebook
- Excel

## Dataset
**IBM Telco Customer Churn Dataset**

The dataset contains customer information, including:
- Customer demographics
- Tenure and contract details
- Internet and additional services
- Payment methods
- Monthly and total charges
- Customer churn status

## Key Analysis
- Customer churn and retention rate analysis.

- Contract and payment method comparisons.

## Business Recommendations
- Encourage long-term subscriptions through loyalty offers.
- Improve technical support for high-risk customer segments.
- Review pricing strategies for customers with higher monthly charges.
- Improve payment convenience and billing experiences.
- Introduce targeted retention campaigns for new customers.
